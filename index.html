About

I'm Mubeen. My background is in accounting and finance, and after a few years spent reconciling transactions and building monthly reports, I realised the part I enjoyed most was the data itself — finding the one discrepancy in 200 transactions, or turning a messy paper trail into something clean and usable. That's what led me into data analytics, and it's still what I enjoy most: making sense of the detail so people can actually use it.

The tools I use most are Excel and Power BI. Excel has been part of every role I've had, and Power BI is how I present findings so they make sense to any audience. I also work with SQL and Python regularly through my training with Generation, and those are the two I'm focused on building up right now. Take a look at my projects, or grab my CV.
